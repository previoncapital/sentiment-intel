**To:** City Clerk's Office, City of Toronto (clerk@toronto.ca — confirm the address before sending)
**Subject:** Request for access and reuse terms — Council and committee records for housing research

Dear City Clerk's Office,

I am [name], [role] of [company]. We are building a research database of
public records about housing and land-use decisions in Toronto since December
2018: Council and committee agendas, decisions and votes, and related
planning, Committee of Adjustment, TLAB and Ontario Land Tribunal records.
Every figure we publish will link back to the City's original record. We
report residents' participation only in aggregate and will not offer any
search by a private individual's name.

We already use the City's open data under the Open Government Licence –
Toronto. Before we go further, we would like to do things properly and ask
for your guidance on the following:

1. **Meeting video.** On what terms may meeting recordings from the City
   Clerk's YouTube channel be downloaded and transcribed for analysis? Could
   the Clerk's Office supply recordings, or the direct audio feed, and under
   what licence?
2. **Agenda and decision data.** Is there a structured feed or bulk export of
   TMMIS meetings, agenda items, decisions and document lists? Our automated
   requests to secure.toronto.ca/council are currently refused (HTTP 403),
   though we identify ourselves and limit our request rate. Is there a
   permitted way for a research project to access these pages, or would you
   prefer we use a different channel?
3. **Reuse of documents.** May we store and quote from staff reports,
   decisions and communications published on toronto.ca/legdocs in a
   commercial research product, with attribution and links to the original?
   If written permission is needed, who should we ask?
4. **Campaign finance.** Are the 2022 and 2026 candidate contribution records
   available as a bulk file, like the 2018 data on the open data portal?

We would be glad to meet, and to share our methods.

Sincerely,
[name]
