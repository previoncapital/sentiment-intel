## Things that didn't add up, and what we found

**1. Two appointed councillors are missing from the City's attendance records.** Robin Buxton Potts (Ward 13, appointed June 1, 2022) and Rose Milczyn (Ward 1, appointed August 15, 2022) voted at Council, but the City's attendance file never lists them. It isn't a spelling problem: after Joe Cressy and Kristyn Wong-Tam left in spring 2022, the number of members listed at each Council session fell from 26 to 24 and stayed there. Joe Mihevc, appointed the same day as Buxton Potts, was added; she and Milczyn never were. All four versions of the file the City publishes (including copies refreshed in February 2026) have the same gap, so it is in the City's source records, not one bad download. Why only these two is not something the data can tell us; it's a question for the City's open data team. *What it means for us:* attendance can't be used on its own to decide who was in office; we use election results and appointments instead.

**2. Ward 22 changed hands five times in under a year, but no meeting was missed.** Jim Karygiannis was removed by the City Clerk on Nov 6, 2019 over campaign spending, put back by a judge on Nov 25, 2019, removed by the Court of Appeal on June 24, 2020, put back temporarily on about Aug 5, 2020 while he asked the Supreme Court to hear his case, and removed for good on Sept 24, 2020 when it declined. The attendance records look as if nothing happened in 2019 and he simply stopped coming in mid-2020. Both fit: no Council meeting fell inside either gap in which he was back in office.

**3. Most 2018–2022 Council votes have no date.** In the City's voting file, 87% of rows for that term have a blank date. Committee votes all have dates; full Council votes have them only for April to November 2020, the first months of virtual meetings. The pattern points to how votes were recorded at Council, not to missing votes. The dates can be filled back in from the City's meeting schedule. From November 2022 on, every Council vote carries the exact time it was taken.

**4. Agenda codes don't use the calendar year.** Items at the first meetings of a new Council (e.g. November and December 2022) are coded with the next year ("2023.…"). Anything matching votes to dates must use the meeting date.

**5. "When did they start?" has more than one answer.** The 2018 Council's term legally began December 1, 2018, but members took their oath at the first meeting on December 4. By-election winners are declared by the Clerk a day or two after the vote and take the oath at the next Council meeting; we use the oath date (or, where only that is known, the first meeting attended) and say which.

**Still unconfirmed:** the exact oath dates for Parthi Kandavel (Ward 20, 2023) and Neethan Shan (Ward 25, 2025), and the date of the August 2020 court stay. These would come from Council minutes once we can read the City's agenda site.
