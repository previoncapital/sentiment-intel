## Data issues found while building this

- The City's **Council attendance dataset leaves out two appointed councillors** (Robin Buxton Potts, Ward 13; Rose Milczyn, Ward 1, both 2022), although both appear in the voting records.
- **Jim Karygiannis's end date (Ward 22, 2020) is unconfirmed**: removal went back and forth in the courts between November 2019 and 2020. The date shown is his last Council meeting.
- The 2018–2022 term start (December 1, 2018) is taken from the election law of the time and still needs a City record to confirm it.
- Agenda item codes are numbered by **council year, not calendar year**: the first meeting of the 2022–26 term (November 2022) has items coded `2023.…`. Linking must use the meeting date, not the year in the code.
